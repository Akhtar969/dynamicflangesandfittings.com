ABOUT THE COMPANY

About Dynamic Flanges and Fittings: FAQs

Quick answers about who we are and how we work. More questions? Contact us.

Who is Dynamic Flanges and Fittings?

Dynamic Flanges and Fittings is an MS flanges manufacturing company in Mumbai. We manufacture and supply MS flanges, pipe fittings and industrial pipes for industrial piping, working from our factory in Darukhana, Mumbai.

Is Dynamic Flanges and Fittings a manufacturer or a trader?

We are a manufacturer and supplier. Products are made at our factory at Gala No. 10, Plot 255, Mehta Lane, Darukhana, Mumbai 400010, and quoted directly from there, so you deal with the maker.

What does Dynamic Flanges and Fittings manufacture?

We make blind, welding neck, slip-on, plate and E table flanges, plus MS fittings such as elbows, tees and reducers, and industrial pipes. Everything is quoted to your drawing, specification or standard.

Where is your factory and registered office?

The factory is at Gala No. 10, Plot 255, Mehta Lane, Darukhana, Mumbai 400010. The registered office is at Shop No. 5, Ground Floor, Qashmi Palace, Kausa, Mumbra 400612. Please contact us before visiting the factory.

Is Dynamic Flanges and Fittings GST registered?

Yes. Our GSTIN is 27ETUPK7095C2Z0. GST invoices are issued for supply, and the GSTIN is also listed in our website footer and contact page.

Who do you supply?

We supply contractors, engineers, project purchasers and industrial buyers in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Pune and the rest of Maharashtra, and welcome enquiries from other states.

Can I get test certificates with my order?

Material Test Certificates and inspection reports can be provided with supply where available. Ask for them in your enquiry. Our certification page explains our inspection approach.

How do I contact Dynamic Flanges and Fittings?

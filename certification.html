VERIFY BEFORE YOU BUY

How to Verify a Flange Supplier’s Registrations

Do not rely on a logo alone. Check the numbers on the official portals, then ask the supplier for the matching document.

  • GST: search the GSTIN 27ETUPK7095C2Z0 on the official GST portal.
  • Udyam: check the registration number on the official Udyam portal.
  • ISO and IEC: ask for the certificate scope, issuing body and validity.
  • Material: ask for MTC that matches the lot being supplied.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Flange Certification FAQs

Quick answers on our registrations, ISO and IEC, MTC and inspection. More questions? Contact us.

What certifications does Dynamic Flanges and Fittings have?

Dynamic Flanges and Fittings is registered under GST and holds an Udyam (MSME) registration. Our parent company holds the ISO certificate and the Import Export Code (IEC). Copies of all four are shown on this page, and Material Test Certificates can be supplied with orders where available.

Is Dynamic Flanges and Fittings ISO certified?

The ISO certificate shown on this page is held by our parent company. We display it for buyer reference. Please ask us for the certificate scope and validity, and tell us if your vendor registration needs a particular document.

What is an Udyam registration certificate?

Udyam registration is the Government of India’s MSME registration, issued by the Ministry of MSME. It confirms a business as a micro, small or medium enterprise. Buyers often ask for it during vendor registration and MSME-linked procurement.

Is Dynamic Flanges and Fittings GST registered?

Yes. Our GSTIN is 27ETUPK7095C2Z0 and the GST registration certificate is shown on this page. You can also check the GSTIN on the official GST portal using the Search Taxpayer option.

What is an IEC and why is it shown here?

An Importer Exporter Code (IEC) is a 10-digit code issued by DGFT that is needed to import or export goods. The IEC shown here is held by our parent company and is displayed so buyers can see the group’s trade registration.

Can you provide Material Test Certificates (MTC) for flanges?

Material Test Certificates and inspection reports can be provided with supply where available. Please request them in your enquiry, along with the material grade and standard you need, so we can arrange them with your order.

How can I verify your certificates?

Check the GSTIN on the GST portal, and check the Udyam registration on the official Udyam portal. For ISO and IEC copies, contact us and we will share the details. Our sales team can also send certificate copies by email or WhatsApp.

What inspection do you do before dispatch?

We verify material against the order, check dimensions against the drawing or standard, and inspect the visual and surface finish before dispatch. Share your drawing or table, such as BS 10 Table E or IS 1538, so we check against it.

Which documents can I request with my enquiry?

You can request GST and Udyam copies, the ISO and IEC copies, MTC and inspection reports where available. Mention the documents in your enquiry or on WhatsApp and we will confirm what we can supply with your order.

Need Certificates With Your Flange Quotation?

Mention the documents you need when you send your flange enquiry and we will confirm what we can supply.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Contact and Enquiry FAQs

Quick answers before you get in touch. Still unsure? Call +91 98920 65723.

How do I contact an MS flanges supplier in Mumbai?

Call or WhatsApp Dynamic Flanges and Fittings on +91 98920 65723, email sales@dynamicflangesandfittings.com, or fill in the enquiry form on this page. Share your product, size, standard and quantity, and we will reply with a quotation.

What information should I send for a flange quotation?

Send the flange type, nominal size, pressure class or table, material, standard or drawing, and quantity. For fittings and pipes, add size, thickness and quantity. A drawing or a clear photo of the old part helps us quote faster.

Can I send my drawing on WhatsApp?

Yes. Fill in the form to open WhatsApp with your enquiry pre-filled, then attach your drawing, photo or specification in the same chat. You can also email it to sales@dynamicflangesandfittings.com.

Where is your factory and can I visit?

Our factory is at Gala No. 10, Plot 255, Mehta Lane, Darukhana, Mumbai 400010. Please call before visiting so we can be ready for you. Our registered office is in Kausa, Mumbra 400612.

Do you supply outside Mumbai?

Yes. We supply Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Pune and the rest of Maharashtra, and welcome enquiries from Gujarat, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bangalore and other states. Mention your delivery location in the enquiry.

Can you quote in bulk and to my drawing?

Yes. Bulk quotations are available for contractors, project purchasers and industrial buyers, and we quote against your drawing, specification or standard such as BS 10 Table E or IS 1538.

Can I ask for test certificates with my quotation?

Yes. Material Test Certificates and inspection reports can be provided with supply where available. Mention them in your enquiry. See our certification page for GST, Udyam, ISO and IEC copies.

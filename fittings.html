TYPES OF MS PIPE FITTINGS

MS Pipe Fitting Types We Supply

As an MS pipe fittings supplier in Mumbai, we supply elbows and bends to turn a line, tees to branch it, reducers to change size, and caps and stub ends to close or flange it. Pick the type, then request a quotation.

Direction change

90° Elbows (LR and SR)

A 90 degree elbow turns the pipeline through a right angle. Long radius (LR) elbows have a radius of 1.5 times the pipe size. Short radius (SR) elbows have a radius of 1 times the pipe size.

Connection

45° Elbows

A 45 degree elbow changes direction by half a right angle. It gives a smoother flow and is used for offsets and gradual turns.

A 180 degree return bend turns the line back on itself. The centre-to-centre distance is set by the radius and pipe size.

PIPE SCHEDULES

Sch 10, Sch 40, Sch 80 and Sch 160 Fittings Explained

Schedule is a number that sets pipe wall thickness. A higher schedule means a thicker wall and higher pressure capacity. A butt weld fitting must match the schedule of the pipe, so always state the schedule when you ask for a quotation.

Schedule Wall thickness Typical use
Sch 10 Thin wall (3.05 mm at 4″) Low-pressure water, air and light service
Sch 40 (STD) Standard wall (6.02 mm at 4″) General industrial piping
Sch 80 (XS) Heavy wall (8.56 mm at 4″) Higher pressure process and steam lines
Sch 160 Very heavy wall (13.49 mm at 4″) High-pressure service

Other schedules such as Sch 20, 30, STD, XS and XXS can be quoted on enquiry. Pipe sizes are shown in the table below.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

MS Pipe Fittings FAQs: Types, Schedules and Dimensions

Quick answers from an MS pipe fittings supplier in Mumbai. Need more? Contact us.

What types of MS pipe fittings do you supply?

We supply MS elbows (90, 45 and 180 degree), long radius bends, equal and reducing tees, concentric and eccentric reducers, end caps and stub ends. Other fittings such as crosses, couplings and forged fittings can be quoted on enquiry.

What is the difference between LR and SR elbows?

A long radius (LR) elbow has a bend radius of 1.5 times the pipe size and gives lower flow resistance. A short radius (SR) elbow has a radius of 1 times the pipe size and fits tight spaces. LR elbows are the standard choice unless space is limited.

What is a pipe schedule?

Schedule is a number that sets the wall thickness of a pipe for a given nominal size. A higher schedule means a thicker wall and higher pressure capacity. A fitting must match the schedule of the pipe it is welded to.

What is the difference between Sch 40 and Sch 80 fittings?

Sch 80 fittings have a thicker wall than Sch 40 and take higher pressure. For a 4 inch fitting, wall thickness is 6.02 mm in Sch 40 and 8.56 mm in Sch 80, with an outside diameter of 114.3 mm. Sch 80 needs closer wall thickness checks.

What are the dimensions of a 4 inch 90 degree elbow?

A 4 inch (DN 100) 90 degree long radius elbow has a centre-to-end dimension of 152 mm. A short radius elbow is 102 mm. A 4 inch 45 degree LR elbow is 64 mm. See the elbow table on this page.

What size range of fittings do you supply?

The dimension tables on this page cover NPS 1 to 12 inch (DN 25 to 300) for butt weld fittings, and pipe wall thickness from NPS ½ to 12 inch. Larger sizes and other schedules can be quoted on enquiry. Send size, schedule and quantity.

Do you manufacture pipe fittings or only supply them?

We manufacture MS flanges at our Darukhana factory and supply MS pipe fittings. Fittings are sourced to your size, schedule and standard, checked on receipt for dimensions, wall thickness and bevel, and dispatched with MTC where available.

What is the bevel angle on butt weld fittings?

Butt weld fittings are normally supplied with a bevelled end of 37.5 degrees, with a small root face, so they can be welded to the pipe. Tell us if your drawing needs a different bevel or a plain end.

Should I choose seamless or ERW fittings?

Seamless fittings suit higher pressure and critical service. ERW fittings suit general industrial piping at a lower cost. Tell us the pressure, temperature and fluid, and we will quote the right option and confirm availability.

What do you need to quote pipe fittings?

Send the fitting type, nominal size, schedule or wall thickness, seamless or ERW, material, bevel and standard or drawing, and quantity. Add MTC if you need test certificates, and we reply with a quotation.

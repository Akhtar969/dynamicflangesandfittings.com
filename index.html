TYPES OF MS FLANGES

Which MS Flange Type Do You Need?

MS flanges differ mainly in how they attach to the pipe. Use this table to match the type to your job, then send us your requirement.

Flange type How it works Typically used for
Blind flange Solid disc that closes the end of a pipe or nozzle Isolating a line, tank and vessel openings, future expansion
Welding neck flange Tapered hub is butt-welded to the pipe Higher pressure and temperature lines, stronger joints
Slip-on flange Slips over the pipe, then welded General piping where easy fitting and alignment matter
Plate flange Flat plate, cut and drilled to size Low-pressure lines, water, ducting, custom drilling
E table flange Drilled to BS 10 Table E Jobs whose drawings specify Table E

Not sure which one you need? Share your pipe size, pressure and application on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.

STANDARDS, TABLES & DIMENSIONS

Flange Dimensions: BS 10 Table D & E, IS 1538 and PN Ratings

Most flange enquiries name a standard or table. Tell us which one your drawing uses and we will quote to it. Dimensions are set by the table and the nominal size.

Standard or table What it defines What to send us
BS 10 Table D Flange thickness, PCD and bolt holes for general low-pressure piping Table D and nominal size
BS 10 Table E Higher-rated drilling and dimensions than Table D Table E and nominal size
IS 1538 tables Indian Standard flange dimension and drilling tables Table number, such as Table 4 or 6, and size
IS 6392 Indian Standard for steel pipe flanges Nominal size and pressure rating
PN 10 / PN 16 Metric pressure ratings used on European-style drawings PN rating and nominal size

What a flange drawing shows

Outside diameter, PCD (bolt circle), thickness, bolt hole size and number of holes, and bore.

No drawing?

Send the nominal size and table name, or a clear photo with measurements. We will confirm the dimensions in your quotation.

Need documents?

Ask for MTC and inspection reports with your enquiry. See our certification page.

We do not publish a fixed dimension chart because values change with table and size. Send your table and size and we will confirm.

MS Flange FAQs: Mumbai Buyer’s Guide

Quick answers on types, tables, materials and how to get a quotation. More questions? Contact us.

How do I choose an MS flange manufacturer in Mumbai?

Choose a manufacturer that works from your drawing or standard, checks material and dimensions before dispatch, can share test certificates on request, and quotes directly from the factory. Dynamic Flanges and Fittings manufactures at Darukhana, Mumbai, so you deal with the maker and not a middleman.

What is an MS flange?

An MS (mild steel) flange is a ring-shaped fitting that joins pipes, valves and equipment with bolts. It lets a piping system be assembled and dismantled for inspection or maintenance. MS flanges suit general industrial piping, water lines and structural work.

What types of MS flanges do you manufacture?

We make blind, welding neck, slip-on, plate and E table flanges. Other types such as socket weld, orifice, lap joint (stub end) or male-female flanges can be quoted on enquiry. Each type differs in how it attaches to the pipe and the pressure it suits.

What is the difference between Table D and Table E flanges?

Table D and Table E are drilling and dimension tables from the British Standard BS 10. They differ in pressure rating, flange thickness and bolt pattern, so they are not interchangeable. Always state the table named on your drawing when you ask for a quotation.

What do I need to share for IS 1538 or Table E flange dimensions?

Tell us the standard and table on your drawing, such as IS 1538 Table 4 or 6 or BS 10 Table E, plus the nominal size and thickness. Outside diameter, PCD and bolt holes depend on the table and size, so we confirm them in your quotation and manufacture to your drawing.

Which MS elbows, bends and reducers can you supply?

We supply MS elbows, tees and reducers. Ask for 45 degree, 90 degree or 180 degree elbows, long radius bends, and concentric or eccentric reducers. Send size, thickness, seamless or ERW preference and quantity, and we will confirm availability.

What information do you need for a flange quotation?

Share the flange type, nominal size, pressure class or table, material, standard or drawing, and quantity. A drawing or a clear photo of the old flange also helps us quote faster and avoid mistakes.

Do you provide Material Test Certificates (MTC)?

Material Test Certificates and inspection reports can be provided with supply where available. Please ask for them when you send your enquiry so we can arrange them with your order.

Can you manufacture flanges to my drawing or specification?

Yes. Send us your drawing or specification and we will quote against it. We then manufacture, check dimensions and surface finish, and dispatch. Bulk quotations are available for contractors and project purchasers.

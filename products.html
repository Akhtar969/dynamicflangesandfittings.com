MATERIAL COMPARISON

MS Flanges vs SS Flanges

MS (mild steel) flanges cost less and suit general industrial piping. SS (stainless steel) flanges resist corrosion better and suit chemical and food services. Pick by the fluid and the environment.

MS (mild steel) SS (stainless steel)
Corrosion resistance Lower; coating or paint often used Higher
Cost Generally lower Generally higher
Fabrication Easy to cut, weld and drill Needs more care when welding and machining
Typical use General industrial piping, water, structural Chemical, food and corrosive services
Best when Budget matters and the fluid is not corrosive The fluid or site is corrosive or hygiene matters

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Flanges, Fittings and Pipes FAQs

Quick answers before you request a quotation. Need more? Contact us.

What products does Dynamic Flanges and Fittings manufacture?

We manufacture and supply MS flanges, MS pipe fittings and industrial pipes from our factory in Darukhana, Mumbai. Flanges include blind, welding neck, slip-on, plate and E table types. Fittings include elbows, tees, reducers and end caps. Pipes include ERW, seamless, black and galvanised.

What is the difference between a flange and a pipe fitting?

A flange is a bolted joint that connects pipes, valves and equipment so a line can be opened for inspection. A pipe fitting, such as an elbow, tee or reducer, changes the direction, branching or size of the line. Most piping jobs need both.

Which flange type should I choose?

Use a blind flange to close a line end, a welding neck flange for higher pressure and temperature, a slip-on flange for general piping that needs easy alignment, and a plate flange for low-pressure lines. Use an E table flange when your drawing names BS 10 Table E.

Which MS pipe fittings do you supply?

We supply elbows, tees, reducers and end caps. You can ask for 45 degree, 90 degree and 180 degree elbows, long radius bends, and concentric or eccentric reducers. Send size, thickness, seamless or ERW preference and quantity, and we will confirm availability.

Which MS pipes do you supply?

We supply ERW, seamless, black and galvanised MS pipes for industrial piping. Round and square pipes can be quoted on enquiry. Sizes, thickness and length are confirmed at quotation, so share your requirement with the quantity.

What is the difference between MS and SS flanges?

MS (mild steel) flanges cost less and suit general industrial piping, water and structural use, usually with paint or coating. SS (stainless steel) flanges resist corrosion better and suit chemical, food and corrosive services, at a higher price.

What details do you need to quote flanges, fittings or pipes?

Send the product type, nominal size, thickness or pressure class, material, standard or drawing, and quantity. For flanges, also name the table, such as BS 10 Table D or E, or IS 1538. A drawing or clear photo of the old part helps us quote faster.

Can you make products to my drawing and supply in bulk?

Yes. Send your drawing or specification and we will quote against it. We manufacture, check dimensions and surface finish, and dispatch. Bulk quotations are available for contractors, project purchasers and industrial buyers.

Do you provide Material Test Certificates (MTC)?

Material Test Certificates and inspection reports can be provided with supply where available. Please ask for them when you send your enquiry so we can arrange them with your order. See our certification page for details.
